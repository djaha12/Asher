Diamonds is the internal business system of the Diamonds jewelry store (Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan). Accounts are issued by the store owner to employees; there is no public sign-up. We intend to request Unlisted App Distribution for this app.

HOW TO REVIEW
The production system holds real customer data, so please use the demo system with sample data:
1. On the sign-in screen tap «Посмотреть демо-версию» (View demo) at the bottom.
2. Sign in with username admin, password admin123 (the same as in the review details). The demo trusts any device, so no owner approval is needed.
3. «Вернуться к рабочей системе» returns to the production sign-in screen.
The demo data is reset every night.

NATIVE FEATURES
- Push notifications: after sign-in the app offers to enable notifications («Разрешить»). Menu → Настройки (Settings) → Безопасность (Security) → «Прислать проверочное» sends a test notification. Owners are notified about sign-in attempts from new devices, recurring expenses coming due and failed nightly backups.
- AirPrint: Menu → Ценники и бирки (Labels) → select items → print sends barcode labels to an AirPrint printer; sale receipts print the same way.
- Camera: Продажи (Sales) or Инвентаризация (Stock check) → scan button reads the barcode on a label.
- Files: «Экспорт CSV» in Sales and Customers opens the iOS share sheet; certificates and photos open in a native viewer.
- Offline: without a connection the app shows its own offline screen and reconnects by itself.
